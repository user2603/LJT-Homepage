I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/hkust-nlp) under the supervision of [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). Previously, I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Research Interests
======
My research interests include:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
